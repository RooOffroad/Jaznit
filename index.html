Drinks logged Target
Targets use the Australian guidelines as a reference: healthy adults should drink no more than 10 standard drinks a week and no more than 4 on any one day (NHMRC 2020, via the Alcohol and Drug Foundation). A standard drink is 10 g of pure alcohol. Each drink is counted as volume in litres × ABV × 0.789 (Cancer Council Victoria). This is a tracking tool, not medical advice. If cutting back feels hard, the National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline is 1800 250 015.
